# Privacy policy

!!! warning "Draft — review before relying on this text"
    This policy is a template for the public docs site. Have it reviewed for your
    jurisdiction and operations before treating it as binding.

**Last updated:** 2026-03-28

Infinite Experiment (“we”, “us”) operates Comrade Bot and Politburo for virtual airlines
using Infinite Flight and Discord.

## What we collect

| Data | Why |
|------|-----|
| Discord user ID and server ID | Identify you in bot commands and API calls |
| IFC username and proof flight | Link your Discord account to your Infinite Flight Community identity without storing your IF password |
| Optional IF API identifiers | Resolve logbook and profile data when you register or sync |
| VA membership, callsign, role | Operate your virtual airline on Discord and in the portal |
| VA configuration you provide | Connect to your datasource (e.g. Airtable) and match live flights |
| Technical logs | Operate, secure, and debug the service |

We do **not** ask for your Infinite Flight password or email as part of registration.

## How we use data

- Provide registration, membership, live views, signed portal links, and APIs your VA enables
- Prevent impersonation and enforce bans decided by platform operators
- Improve reliability and security of the service

## Sharing

We use third-party services you already use:

- **Discord** — bot interactions and identity
- **Infinite Flight** — public/community and API data for verification and live game features
- **Your VA’s datasource** (e.g. Airtable) — when administrators connect it; governed by your VA’s policies

We do not sell personal data.

## Retention

We keep account and membership data while your account is active or as needed to operate the
service. Operator actions (such as ban and delete) may archive a minimal export for disputes
before removing live membership data.

## Your choices

- You can stop using the bot and ask your VA administrator to remove your membership.
- Platform-level deletion and IFC disputes are handled by platform operators, not VA staff alone.

## Contact

For privacy requests, contact the Infinite Experiment operator through your usual VA or
project support channel.
